Optimize liquidity and fund growth

Leverage your short- and long-term assets to optimize liquidity and fund growth including organic growth, mergers and acquisitions, recapitalization and restructuring. Underwritings focus on the value of the borrower's assets as well as its cash flow, allowing asset-based transactions to be structured with less restrictive covenant packages than traditional commercial loans. This structure makes asset-based financing attractive to companies with rapid growth, inconsistent earnings, seasonality, and cyclicality. Citizens specializes in financing to retailers, manufacturers, wholesalers, staffing, distribution and logistics companies.

## Checking Account FAQs

+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Accordion-Faq**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| How do I open a checking account?                                                                                              | Depending on your preference, you can apply for a Citizens checking account online, over the phone, or at any [Citizens branch](https://www.citizensbank.com/customer-service/branch-locator.aspx). To [apply online](https://secureopen.citizensbank.com/), just select the appropriate link from the top of this page and find the account you wish to open. You will then see instructions about applying online. |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| How do I avoid overdrawing my checking account?                                                                                | Knowing how much money is available in your account is critical to avoiding overdraft situations. Citizens gives you tools that make it easy, such as [Online and Mobile Banking](https://www.citizensbank.com/mobile-and-online-banking/overview.aspx)<sup>3</sup>, and account alerts. Learn how to manage overdrafts and avoid fees by viewing our [Overdraft Choices](https://www.citizensbank.com/checking/overdraft.aspx). |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| What is the CitizensPlus* loyalty program?                                                                                     | [CitizensPlus](https://www.citizensbank.com/features/citizensplus.aspx)® is a benefit-rich loyalty program available only with [Citizens Quest](https://www.citizensbank.com/checking/quest-checking-account.aspx)® Checking and Citizens [Private Client](https://www.citizensbank.com/checking/private-client-checking-account.aspx)® Checking accounts. As your relationship with Citizens grows, you get more benefits, rewards, and personalized advice. No additional enrollment is needed. |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| How do I set up direct deposit and what kind of information is required?                                                       | There are two options for [setting up direct deposit](https://www.citizensbank.com/checking/direct-deposit-service.aspx) online or with a direct deposit form from your employer. You will need to provide the following: [Our bank routing number](https://www.citizensbank.com/learning/citizens-bank-routing-number.aspx). This nine-digit number is found on the bottom left of your checks. Your checking account number. It can be found on the bottom of the check directly to the right of the routing number. A voided check. This will allow the employer to verify the above information. Write "VOID" in large letters in ink across the check, covering most lines so that nothing else can be written in on the check. Your branch address. You can find this information online, but you could also call a representative. [More information on direct deposit](https://www.citizensbank.com/checking/direct-deposit-service.aspx). No checks? No problem you can find your routing number online. Still have questions? More [information on routing numbers](https://www.citizensbank.com/learning/citizens-bank-routing-number.aspx). |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| How do I upgrade my account to Citizens Quest® or Citizens Private Client® Checking?                                           | Go into your [nearest branch](https://www.citizensbank.com/customer-service/branch-locator.aspx) or call [1-877-360-2472](tel:1-877-360-2472) |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| How do I update my name and address in the system                                                                              | Simply call us at [1-800-922-9999](tel:1-800-922-9999), visit any [Citizens branch](https://www.citizensbank.com/customer-service/branch-locator.aspx), or use the Change of Address feature of [Online Banking](https://www.citizensbankonline.com/olb-root/login). |

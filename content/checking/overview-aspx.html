How do I open a checking account?

Depending on your preference, you can apply for a Citizens checking account online, over the phone, or at any Citizens branch. To apply online, just select the appropriate link from the top of this page and find the account you wish to open. You will then see instructions about applying online.

How do I avoid overdrawing my checking account?

Knowing how much money is available in your account is critical to avoiding overdraft situations. Citizens gives you tools that make it easy, such as Online and Mobile Banking, and account alerts. Learn how to manage overdrafts and avoid fees by viewing our Overdraft Choices.

What is the CitizensPlus loyalty program?

CitizensPlus® is a benefit-rich loyalty program available only with Citizens Quest® Checking and Citizens Private Client® Checking accounts. As your relationship with Citizens grows, you get more benefits, rewards, and personalized advice. No additional enrollment is needed.

How do I set up direct deposit and what kind of information is required?

There are two options for setting up direct deposit online or with a direct deposit form from your employer. You will need to provide your bank routing number, checking account number, a voided check, and your branch address.

How do I upgrade my account to Citizens Quest® or Citizens Private Client® Checking?

Go into your nearest branch or call 1-877-360-2472

How do I update my name and address in the system

Simply call us at 1-800-922-9999, visit any Citizens branch, or use the Change of Address feature of Online Banking.

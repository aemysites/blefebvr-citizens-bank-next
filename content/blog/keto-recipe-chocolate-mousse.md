# KETO RECIPE: Chocolate Mousse

This easy dessert is rich, chocolatey, and super indulgent. It is packed with fats and bursting with creamy chocolate flavor. It takes less than 5 minutes to make and will cure all of those chocolate cravings!

Serves 2

### INGREDIENTS

- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream

- 2 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder

- 4 tablespoons cream cheese, room temperature

- 3 tablespoon confectioners erythritol (I use [Swerve Confectioners](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0079OYIFS?ref=exp_ketointhecity__dp_vv_d))

- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

- Pinch of salt

### DIRECTIONS

1. Add all ingredients into mixing bowl, turn mixer on high and mix 2 minutes or until light and fluffy. You can serve immediately or divide into 2 small cups and refrigerate.

2. Top with whipped cream if you would like, I also like to add a little shaved chocolate or berries.
